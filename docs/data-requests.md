Ask for aggregated counts only (no personal data), so data protection
is not an obstacle.

## What to ask for

1. **ATM:** T-mobilitat validations per station or stop, per hour, per fare title
   (at least T-usual vs T-casual vs single), Jan 2023 – latest, zone 1.
   Even one representative month helps: it gives the hourly profile and the
   share of each title.
2. **TMB:** written permission to use the ridership spreadsheets
   (metro per station, bus per line, 2021–2025) in the project, and
   whether hourly entries per station can be shared for research.
